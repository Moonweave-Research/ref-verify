ref-verify

Check every reference in your list

Paste a reference list or choose a .bib, .ris, or .txt file. Each reference is compared with its CrossRef record: title, first author, and year.

One reference per line or numbered item. BibTeX and RIS can be pasted too.

or This file is checked instead of the box above.

Your references go from this browser straight to CrossRef and doi.org. There is no ref-verify server: nothing is uploaded to us or stored, and there are no analytics.
