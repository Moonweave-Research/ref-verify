# Interface-controlled magnetism and plasma diagnostics for spintronic thin films

## Introduction

Perpendicular magnetic anisotropy (PMA) in thin films can be tuned by thickness, annealing, and electric fields. In Ta/GdFeCo/Ta heterostructures, PMA dominates near room temperature only in films thinner than 90 nm [1]. CoFe/Fe stacks deposited at 100 K reached a voltage-controlled magnetic anisotropy coefficient of 61 fJ/Vm after annealing at 673 K [2], and lithium-ion migration in TiO2/CoNi suppressed the remanence and coercivity to zero within a voltage window of 1.5 kV [3]. Annealed [CoFeB/Pd]×5 multilayers reached an effective anisotropy of about 7.82 × 10⁵ erg/cc [4], spin spirals can be customized in ferromagnetic films [12], and voltage-gated skyrmion nucleation has been reported in CoFeB/Pd multilayers [11]. Film-thickness effects on optical constants have also been analysed for Ge–Se–Zn chalcogenide films [10].

## Deposition plasma diagnostics

Plasma diagnostics use Ar I line-intensity ratios for electric-field measurement [5], metastable-neutral measurements [6], and electron temperature measurements in dielectric barrier discharges [7]. Beamline characterization with coherent X-rays [8] and polarization-dependent X-ray absorption [9] complete the toolset.
