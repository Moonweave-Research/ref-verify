# Electroactive PVDF and liquid crystal elastomer fibres for soft artificial muscles

## Introduction

Piezoelectric PVDF and liquid crystal elastomers (LCEs) are complementary materials for soft actuators. In PVDF, the β-phase content reached 89.9% in electrospun fibres compared with 78.9% in solvent-cast membranes [6]; hot pressing improves β-phase ordering in 3D-printed PVDF [2], recycled PVDF can be precipitated as high-β granules [3], and rGO-reinforced PVDF-HFP composites harvest energy under hand tapping [1]. Silane-functionalized nanocomposites have been used to tune superabsorbent polymer matrices [8].

For LCEs, optical-fibre actuators driven by an 808 nm laser kept their maximum surface temperature above 48 °C during actuation [4], and cytocompatible spun LCE fibres performed mass-specific work of up to 13.2 J/kg [5]. Bistable LCE metastructures switch beam direction with zero holding power [7], corona-poled PVDF/LCE bilayer fibres combine actuation with self-sensing [9], and polymorphic self-poisoning was recently described in poly(lactic acid) crystallization [10].
