# Durability of mRNA vaccine protection in a regional hospital cohort

## Introduction

The SARS-CoV-2 virus shares 96% whole-genome identity with a bat coronavirus [3], and the first clinical series from Wuhan described severe pneumonia in hospitalised adults [4]. Two mRNA vaccines were authorised within a year. In its pivotal trial, BNT162b2, given as two doses of 30 mg each, was 85% effective in preventing Covid-19 [1], and mRNA-1273 showed 94.1% efficacy [2]. Early observational claims that hydroxychloroquine increased in-hospital mortality [5] shaped treatment protocols during the same period. Genome engineering tools [8,10] and protein structure prediction [7] have since accelerated antigen design, and machine-learning screening is being evaluated in other clinical pathways [11].

## Methods and results

Antibody titres were measured by ELISA, and spike transcript levels by RT-qPCR analysed with the 2^−ΔΔCT method [6]. Among 412 vaccinated staff, breakthrough infection rates rose after month 6, in line with the waning reported in long-term cohorts [9].
